# Procedure: how this skill grades a plan package

<!--
THIS IS THE PART YOU WRITE, and it's new this unit. In units 1 and 2,
SKILL.md held a numbered workflow and you wrote only the judgment
files. Now SKILL.md says "execute procedure.md", and this file holds
the steps Claude follows to grade a plan.

A complete procedure lets someone who has never seen a plan before (a
classmate, or Claude) grade one exactly the way you would. Start from
your group's worksheet: copy each "Our procedure" box under the
heading named in its note below. Anywhere your group knew WHAT to
decide but not HOW to find the evidence needs a step here.

Write steps, not intentions: "read the repro evidence before the plan,
and note what behavior it pins down" is a step; "understand the
context" is not.
-->

## Read order

<!-- Worksheet box: "What to read first".
What gets read, in what order, before any check is graded, and
what to note down from each part while reading. A complete procedure
decides the order (issue first? repro evidence first?) and says why
the order matters for the checks that come later. -->

## Evidence gathering

<!-- Worksheet box: "How to gather the evidence".
For each evidence family your rubric's checks name, the concrete
gathering move: which part of the package (or, live, which page or
thread location per your evidence guide) to pull the fact from, and
what to record. A complete procedure leaves no check whose evidence a
grader would have to hunt for. -->

## Check execution

<!-- Worksheet box: "How to grade each check".
How one check runs against gathered evidence: in what order the
checks execute, what a grader does when evidence for a check is
genuinely absent, and when a check may be graded without re-reading
the whole package. A complete procedure makes two graders grade the
same package the same way. -->

## Verdict assembly

<!-- Worksheet box: "How to reach the verdict".
How the per-check grades become the final accept or reject:
apply your rubric's verdict rule, state how unclear grades enter it,
and say what gets quoted in the output for the deciding check. A
complete procedure produces the same verdict from the same grades,
every time. -->
